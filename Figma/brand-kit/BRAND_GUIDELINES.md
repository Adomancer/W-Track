# W-Track Brand Guidelines — Track Weave (Final Mark)

## 1. Selected Brand Mark
**Track Weave** is W-Track's primary geometric emblem: two `15°`-splayed account tracks joined smoothly through a continuous `W` rhythm, representing bank and e-wallet transaction streams converging cleanly into a verified local ledger.

- **Master Geometry (`256 × 256` viewBox)**: Pure closed circular-arc ribbon (`32`-unit uniform stroke weight, outer height `52.1..202.0`, outer width `13.8..242.2`).
- **Master & Variants (`Figma/brand-kit/`)**:
  - `wtrack-track-weave-master.svg` / `-black.svg`: One-colour black proof (`#000000`)
  - `wtrack-track-weave-white.svg`: Reversed mark for dark & emerald tiles (`#FFFFFF`)
  - `wtrack-track-weave-mono-0b6e63.svg`: Light-mode brand emerald (`#0B6E63`)
  - `wtrack-track-weave-mono-55d9c7.svg`: Dark-mode mint accent (`#55D9C7`)
  - `wtrack-track-weave-square.svg` & `-favicon.svg`: Centered icon & web favicon masters
  - `wtrack-track-weave-app-icon.svg`: Rounded-square app tile (`#0B6E63`, mark scaled `68%`)

## 3. Clear Space, Scaling & Adaptive Icon Rules
- **Minimum Clear Space**: Keep at least `0.25 ×` the mark's width clear on all four sides around the symbol and horizontal/stacked lockups.
- **Minimum Sizes**:
  - Standalone digital symbol: **`16 px`**
  - In-app header / badge (`WtrackBrandMark`): **`24 dp`** inside **`30 dp`** rounded container (`9 dp` corner radius)
  - Android Adaptive Launcher safe zone: centered at **`66 dp`** inside the **`108 × 108 dp`** viewport (`~61%` fill), keeping the `15°` outer arms clear of circle/squircle masks.
- **Typography Lockup**: Pair with **Noto Sans / Sans-Serif Bold** (`W-Track`, tight `-0.2sp` optical tracking).

---

## 4. Misuse Rules (Do Not)
- Do **not** rotate, skew, stretch, or convert the closed ribbon into thin outlines.
- Do **not** drop shadows, glows, or multi-stop gradients onto the symbol itself.
- Do **not** place `#0B6E63` directly onto `#0D1413` without switching to `#55D9C7` or `#FFFFFF` for contrast.
- Do **not** stack `#FFFFFF` onto `#F4F7F6` or `#E2F7EE` without a dark/emerald container.
